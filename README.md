# Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis

## Overview
Centered around uncovering patterns in customer spending, product preferences, subscription behaviour, and purchasing habits to support data backed business decisions. Explore customer shopping behaviour using transactional data from 3K+ purchases across multiple product categories.


## Problem Statement
Retail businesses generate large volumes of customer transaction data, but extracting meaningful insights from this information can be challenging. This task aims to analyze customer demographics, purchasing behaviour, and product performance to identify opportunities for improving customer retention, revenue growth, and marketing strategies.

## Dataset Summary
- Records: 3900 Customer Transactions
- Features: 18

## Key Attributes
- Customer Demographics (Age | Gender | Location), Subscription Status, Product Categories, Purchase Amount, Purchase Frequency, Discount Usage, Shipping Type, Review Ratings, Seasonal Purchasing Trends.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python (Pandas, NumPy), Jupyter Notebook, MySQL, MySQL Workbench, Microsoft PowerBI (Power Query, DAX)

## Project Workflow:

### Step 1: Data Cleaning & Preparation 
- Imported and explored the dataset using Python(Pandas)
- Handled missing values in review ratings using category-wise median imputation
- Standardized column names using snake_case conventions
- Created age-group segments for demographic analysis
- Generated additional features for customer behaviour analysis
- Removed redundant columns after consistency checks
- Loaded the cleaned dataset into MySQL for further analysis

### Step 2: Business Analysis - Insights
Performed SQL analysis to answer key business questions:
- Revenue generated by gender
- Revenue contribution by age groups
- Subscriber vs non-subscriber spending comparison
- High-spending discount users
- Top-rated products
- Discount dependency across products
- Customer segmentation analysis
- Shipping type comparison
- Repeat buyer behaviour analysis
- Product performance within categories

### Step 3: Dashboarding
Developed an intuitive  dashboard to track down:
- Revenue trends
- Customer demographics
- Product category performance
- Subscription behaviour
- Customer segments
- Discount usage patterns
- Shipping preferences

Dashboard Features:
1. Executive Dashboard
- Total Revenue, Average Purchase Value, Total Customers, Subscription Percentage, Discount Usage Percentage

2. Customer Behaviour Analysis
- Revenue by Gender, Revenue by Age Group, Customer Segmentation, Repeat Buyers vs Subscribers, Purchase Frequency Analysis

3. Product Performance
- Revenue by Category, Top Products by Rating, Product-wise Revenue Analysis, Discount Dependency Analysis
4. Operational Insights
- Shipping Type Comparison, Review Rating Analysis, Customer Purchase Patterns



## Key Business Insights:

1. Subscribers Generated Higher Revenue
- Customers enrolled in subscription programs contributed significantly to overall revenue and showed stronger purchasing consistency.
- Subscription programs increase customer retention and lifetime value, making them a valuable long-term growth strategy.

2. Customer Revenue Varies Across Age Groups
- Certain age groups contributed a disproportionately larger share of total revenue compared to others.
- Businesses can focus marketing campaigns and personalized offers on the most profitable customer segments.

3. Repeat Customers Drive Business Growth
- Returning and loyal customers accounted for a substantial portion of purchases and revenue.
- Customer retention initiatives often provide higher returns than acquiring new customers and can significantly improve profitability.

4. Discounts Influence Purchasing Behaviour
- Several products showed a high percentage of discounted purchases, indicating that customers were more likely to buy them when promotions were available.
- While discounts can boost sales volume, excessive dependence on discounts may reduce profit margins and create pricing sensitivity.

5. Top-Rated Products Represent Growth Opportunities
- Certain products consistently received high customer ratings and positive reviews.
- Promoting highly rated products can improve customer satisfaction, increase conversion rates, and strengthen brand perception.

6. Shipping Preferences Impact Customer Spending
- Customers using faster shipping options tended to demonstrate different spending patterns compared to standard shipping users.
- Understanding shipping preferences can help optimize logistics strategies and improve customer experience.


## Business Recommendations:
- Expand subscriber-exclusive offers to increase customer retention.
- Implement loyalty programs to convert returning customers into loyal customers.
- Review discount strategies to balance sales growth and profitability.
- Promote top-rated products through targeted marketing campaigns.
- Focus customer acquisition efforts on high-revenue demographic segments.
- Optimize shipping options based on customer preferences and purchasing behaviour.


## Conclusion
This project demonstrates the complete analytics lifecycle, from raw data preparation to business intelligence reporting. By combining Python, SQL, and Power BI, the analysis provides actionable insights into customer behaviour, purchasing patterns, and revenue drivers, enabling data-driven decision-making.
